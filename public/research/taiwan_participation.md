# Taiwan participation: what is established and what still needs an answer

Reviewed 20 October 2026. No individual eligibility determination was obtained. Being based in Hong Kong does not establish the artist’s citizenship, travel-document category or right to conduct retail activity in Taiwan.

## Entry and on-site activity

Taiwan’s National Immigration Agency maintains a distinct [Hong Kong/Macau short-stay route](https://servicestation.immigration.gov.tw/5385/7244/7250/7296/%E5%81%9C%E7%95%99/) (S057; indexed official page). The research did **not** establish whether the user’s proposed personal booth operation and direct sales are permitted under their entry category or require another authorization.

Before committing to travel, ask the NIA/appropriate representative office about the exact activity: a Hong Kong-based individual entering with approximately40 original vinyl collectibles, attending an exhibition and personally receiving payments. State the actual passport/residency category. Mainland-resident business-visit rules surfaced in searches but were not applied automatically to Hong Kong residents.

## Toys in luggage and import inspection

[Taipei Customs passenger guidance](https://web.customs.gov.tw/taipei/singlehtml/3392?cntId=cus2_3392_3392_1352) (S054; published8 August2025) explains that qualifying inspection exemptions for personal articles, commercial samples, exhibits and research items are restricted to **non-sale use**. It directs relevant passengers to the red declaration/consultation channel.

Consequently, do not assume40 units intended for sale can enter under a personal-use or non-sale exhibition exemption. Ask Customs/BSMI or a local importer how to declare the goods, their value and any units brought back unsold. Whether hand-carrying is permitted and what paperwork is needed remain product-specific.

[BSMI’s toy-safety notice](https://www.bsmi.gov.tw/SafetyGIP/wSite/ct?ctNode=8274&mp=65&xItem=128619) (S056) describes the children’s-toy inspection framework. The research did not establish a classification ruling for this sofubi product. Intended age, presentation, use and product characteristics need review; simply describing it as an adult collectible should not be treated as a verified exemption.

## Sales tax, receipts and the local selling party

[Ministry of Finance exhibition guidance](https://www.etax.nat.gov.tw/etwmain/tax-info/understanding/tax-knowledge/jaAYbQw) (S055; updated11 August2025) describes reporting responsibilities for organizers and exhibitors, including unregistered participants and exhibition sales. It is not a complete answer for this nonresident artist.

Ask the organizer which entity is the seller/importer of record, how sales are reported, and who issues receipts/invoices. Confirm any commission, service charge and settlement timing in writing. A shop selling on consignment may change the operating arrangement, but does not automatically eliminate import or tax obligations.

The ARK forms mention invoice assistance without verified pricing. TIF’s rules contain conflicting statements about organizer assistance; its FAQ quotes payment/invoice services while another section disclaims help. Neither is a substitute for confirming a workable arrangement for a Hong Kong individual.

## Practical decision

For a first40-unit release, secure a written answer on **acceptance, total booth cost, entry/activity permission, import handling and sales reporting** before booking Taiwan travel or paying a nonrefundable booth charge. This is directly relevant to feasibility; no verified budget or legal clearance is implied by a visible application form.
