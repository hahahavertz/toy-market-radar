## Part E — Community entry strategy

Prepare a compact introduction for these contacts: photos of both colourways, dimensions/material, original character concept, edition quantity, intended price and a tabletop display. This is an analyst recommendation, not a claim that every organizer requires this packet.

Use local conversations around **CODE CORNER, Angel Abby, ATS and TCM** to establish audience fit and ask about their smallest suitable participation format. A future curated show or retail proposal may suit40 units, but availability must be confirmed.

In Taiwan, **Fairycookies' explicit proposal invitation** and **ARK's real waitlists** are concrete starting points. Monster Taipei/BlackMonster representation and Team Sofucon's historical participation show how artists have used networks; they do not imply current recruitment.

Compare full fixed costs with the batch's achievable contribution before committing. Keep booth fee, travel, accommodation, imports, receipt/tax handling and commissions separate. No sales forecast or acceptance probability is supplied.

Read `taiwan_participation.md` before committing to Taiwan. Official sources distinguish non-sale exhibition samples from sales stock, and entry/activity permission plus tax handling remain unresolved for this individual.
